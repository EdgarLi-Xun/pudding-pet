# Look mechanics — Buding

## Natural motion question
What is the best natural motion for this character when looking around?

Buding is a compact red Shiba Inu with a distinct head, upright triangular ears, dark almond physical eyes, a black nose, and a thick curled tail. The lower body (chest harness + planted fore/hind stance) should stay anchored while the head/neck leads the gaze.

## Anchors and leaders
- **Anchored:** feet/base, lower torso, lavender harness chest piece stay mostly stable.
- **Leads gaze:** whole head turn/tilt plus both physical eyeballs rotating in their sockets (sclera + iris + pupil move together). Nose tip follows head yaw.
- **Follow-through:** ears tip slightly toward the look direction; curled tail may shift a little opposite or lag; upper shoulders lean subtly.
- **Props:** mint-teal harness buckle stays attached and mostly body-locked; laptop is NOT part of look rows (look rows use base identity only).

## Eye mechanics
Physical eyeballs: rotate whole eye globes. Do not slide pupils alone across fixed eye whites. Do not paint replacement googly eyes.

## Cardinal pose families (viewer/screen coordinates)
- **000 up:** head tilts up, eyes rotate upward, nose tip rises; body stays planted.
- **090 screen-right:** head yaws right; nose tip and pupils clearly on the right side of head center; left cheek becomes more visible.
- **180 down:** head tilts down, eyes rotate downward, nose tip drops.
- **270 screen-left:** head yaws left; nose tip and pupils clearly on the left side of head center; right cheek becomes more visible.

## Motion budget
Each 22.5° step moves head/eyes/ears by roughly equal visual amounts. No whole-sprite rotation, skew, or affine tilt. Diagonals interpolate adjacent cardinal families.

## Avoid
Whole-body turntable spin, pupil-only sliding, new eye designs, detached effects, shadows, scenery, text, guide marks.
